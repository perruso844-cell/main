# Common App Activities and Honors

A different skill from essays: dense, factual, scannable. Readers spend seconds
per entry.

## Limits (confirm in the portal)
- **Activities:** up to 10.
  - Position/leadership: 50 characters
  - Organization name: 100 characters
  - Description: 150 characters (spaces count)
  - Plus grade levels, timing, hours/week, weeks/year
- **Honors:** up to 5, title 100 characters each, plus grade levels and level of recognition (school, state/regional, national, international).

Check lengths with `python3 scripts/activity_check.py essays/activities.md`.

## Ordering
List by importance to the student, not by date. Lead with the activities that
show the most commitment, impact, or leadership. Jobs and family responsibilities
(caring for siblings, working to support the family) count and should be listed.

## Writing the description (150 characters)
Formula: **action verb + what you did + scale/result.**
- Start with a strong verb. Drop "I" and articles where it still reads naturally.
- Use numbers: people, dollars, hours, events, growth, rankings.
- Show impact or outcome, not just duties.
- Don't repeat the position or organization name; those are separate fields.
- Semicolons or commas between items are fine; full sentences aren't needed.
- Abbreviations are OK if they're clear (e.g., "&", "hrs", "HS"). Don't make it cryptic.

### Before and after
| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| I am a member of the robotics club and I help build robots for competitions. | Designed drivetrain for 18-member team; robot placed 4th of 62 at state; taught CAD to 9 new members. |
| Volunteered at the food bank. | Sorted & packed 2,000+ lbs of food monthly; launched Saturday teen shift that grew from 3 to 25 volunteers. |
| Worked at a restaurant. | Line cook 20 hrs/wk during school; trained 4 new hires; trusted to close kitchen alone by 2nd month. |
| Took care of my siblings. | Get 2 younger brothers to school, cook dinner & supervise homework daily while parents work evening shifts. |

These are invented illustrations of the style, not anyone's real entries. When
coaching, only use the student's real numbers and results; ask for them
instead of estimating.

## Position field (50 characters)
Be specific: "Captain (11–12); Varsity Starter (10)" rather than "Member."
Show progression across years when there is one.

## Honors (100 characters)
Name the award exactly, then add the scale if the name doesn't explain it:
"National Merit Commended Student", "AIME Qualifier (top ~5% of AMC 12 takers)".
Don't pad with minor certificates; fewer real honors read better.

## Additional Information overflow
If an activity needs context that won't fit (an unusual program, a long-term
project, why hours dropped), a sentence or two in Additional Information is fine.
Don't use it to list more activities.
