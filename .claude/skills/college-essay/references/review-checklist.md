# Review Checklist

## Big picture
- [ ] Answers the actual prompt (all parts of it)
- [ ] One-sentence summary of what the essay is about is clear
- [ ] Reader learns something about the student not elsewhere in the application
- [ ] Passes the swap test (no one else could have written it)
- [ ] The student is the main character (not a parent, coach, or grandparent)
- [ ] Shows a change, insight, or way of thinking — not just events

## Opening
- [ ] Starts with something concrete and specific
- [ ] Avoids: dictionary definitions, famous quotes, "Ever since I was little…", rhetorical questions, "Imagine…"
- [ ] Doesn't spend the first paragraph on setup

## Body
- [ ] Specific details (names, numbers, sensory detail, dialogue) instead of abstractions
- [ ] Each paragraph earns its place
- [ ] Transitions are logical; time jumps are clear

## Reflection and ending
- [ ] Reflection is genuine and specific, not "This taught me the value of hard work"
- [ ] Ending doesn't just summarize or moralize
- [ ] Ending connects back to the opening or opens forward

## Voice
- [ ] Sounds like a real teenager talking thoughtfully
- [ ] No inflated vocabulary ("plethora," "myriad," "utilize," "tapestry," "delve")
- [ ] Humor/personality kept where it's natural
- [ ] Doesn't read as AI-generated: no "a testament to," "in today's world," "not only… but also" stacks, triplet lists everywhere, em-dash overuse

## Line level
- [ ] Cut filler: "very," "really," "just," "in order to," "the fact that," "I think," "I feel"
- [ ] Prefer active voice and strong verbs
- [ ] Vary sentence length
- [ ] No clichés: "comfort zone," "passion," "make a difference," "opened my eyes," "rollercoaster," "it's not about winning"

## Mechanics
- [ ] Within word limit
- [ ] Spelling, grammar, punctuation checked
- [ ] Correct school name (in supplements!)
- [ ] Read aloud once — awkward spots become obvious

## Giving feedback
Format: (1) what's working, (2) top 3 priorities, (3) line notes quoting the text.
Ask questions to draw out missing specifics instead of supplying them.
